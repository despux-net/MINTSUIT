A story of desire, mystery, and a late-night connection.

Sublime tells the story of a special night and the attraction between two people, unfolding step by step through its six tracks.

It opens with «Sex & Velvet» — the first attraction, that feeling that something intense is about to start. The tension rises through «Hungry Lips» and «Y2Ks Eyes», with its magnetic, retro-inspired look. «Redamancy» is the moment the feeling is returned. «Pick You Up At 8» is driving away together, the shared excitement. And «Divine» closes the night with that peaceful satisfaction after it hits its absolute highest peak.

The Record So Far

MINT SUIT has been making mixes since 2013 — though not under that name. There were others before it: handles that surfaced on a single upload and were gone by the next, each one set down as quietly as it had been picked up.

Almost nothing from those years survives. The files went down with the sites that hosted them, and what remains is catalogued as lost media — a short list of titles that still circulates, remembered by a few people and confirmed by no one. None of the audio has ever resurfaced.

Between 2018 and 2024, without hurry and without an audience, the six tracks that became Sublime were assembled.

MINT SUIT has never treated any of this as a career. The music is a hobby, made late and released only once it is finished — which is why, after more than a decade, so little of it exists at all. Sublime is the first work MINT SUIT has agreed to keep.
